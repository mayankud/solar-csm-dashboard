Clear definitions. Trustworthy signals.

New paid service orders

Count each accepted, paid service order once by installer, service and submitted date. Revisions are separate events. Show cancellations and refunds separately; a project may contain several service orders.

Matched-period order pace

(Current MTD orders ÷ prior-month orders through the same business-day count − 1) × 100. Use an agreed US business calendar. Show “New / no baseline” when the denominator is zero.

Transparent health rules for this prototype

At risk: pace falls at least 20% and at least 5 orders, or ordering silence exceeds 2× normal cadence with a baseline of at least 10 orders. Watchlist: pace falls at least 10% and at least 3 orders, or on-time delivery is below 90%, or hold share exceeds 20%. Otherwise healthy. Highest triggered severity wins. These are proposed thresholds, not industry benchmarks.

Gap value is an estimate

max(0, matched prior-period orders − MTD orders) × sample net service fee. This is the value of the observed order gap, not booked revenue loss or a month-end forecast.

Data quality before scoring

In production, stale feeds, missing company mappings or incomplete order coverage should produce “Unknown / review data,” not a healthy score. Match contacts using verified installer IDs; exclude confirmed test records and distinguish hold history from active holds.

Team handoff

Connect order history, service fees, milestone timestamps, hold reasons, verified company IDs and CSM ownership. Retain source timestamps and evidence links. This prototype uses embedded fictional data; filters and downloads run locally, with no connection to the live CSM system.
