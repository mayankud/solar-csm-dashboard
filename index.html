What to do with a signal

Activity slows down

Confirm the customer's own demand, seasonality and share of work before assuming a competitor won it.

Delivery or a critical issue deteriorates

Agree an accountable resolution owner and date. Validate the outcome with the customer.

The customer becomes quiet

Check whether the pause is expected. Review the decision-maker relationship and the next buying milestone.

Measure recovery, not just closure

  1. Issue resolvedCustomer-confirmed resolution, with supporting evidence.
  2. Activity resumesA new order, meaningful usage or other agreed success event.
  3. Recovery holdsActivity returns to the agreed baseline for a full observation period.

This template does not infer recovery from a closed ticket, refund or edited health score.
